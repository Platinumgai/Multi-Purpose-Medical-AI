# Multi-Purpose Medical AI

## 🚀 Overview

Multi-Purpose Medical AI is an intelligent healthcare platform that leverages Agentic AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Digital Health Technologies to provide early disease screening, diagnostics, patient engagement, and healthcare accessibility.

The platform is designed to support community-level healthcare by enabling AI-powered symptom analysis, disease prediction, digital medical records, doctor appointment booking, and multilingual healthcare assistance.

Our solution primarily addresses **Community Screening & Diagnostics (Theme 1)** while also supporting **Disease Management & Patient Engagement (Theme 2)**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Healthcare systems face several challenges:

* Delayed disease diagnosis
* Lack of affordable screening solutions
* Difficulty managing medical records
* Limited healthcare accessibility in rural areas
* Language barriers between patients and healthcare providers
* Inefficient appointment booking processes

This project aims to solve these challenges through a unified AI-driven healthcare ecosystem.
